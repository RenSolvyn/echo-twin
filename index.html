Echo Twin

Map the room around you with sound. No camera. Works offline.

Plays short high chirps and listens to their echoes. Nothing leaves your phone.

Two permissions

  • Microphone to hear the echoes of the chirps.
  • Motion to know which way the phone points while you turn.

Audio stays on this phone. You can stop at any time.

Field test

  
Measure from the spot you turned on (between your feet) straight out to each wall, in the order you turned past them. The numbers are saved with the recording.

  
  
  
  
  

  





  
Tape calibration

  
Every distance from one scan shares the same small errors (air temperature, where the speaker and microphone sit). Tape one wall and all walls, the floor and the ceiling are corrected. Two walls at clearly different distances also fix the scale.

Limits and how it works

  
The phone chirps every 0.12 s while you turn on the spot. Each wall echoes at a range that rises and falls as the phone swings around you; that pattern gives the wall's direction and distance. The floor and ceiling are found while you raise the phone.

  
Walls drawn dashed and amber are inferred from weak evidence. Colours of measured walls go from cyan (within a few cm) to yellow (less certain).

  
Radar mode alternates up and down sweeps. A moving person shifts the two in opposite directions, which gives their speed toward or away from you. One phone measures distance and speed, not the exact direction of a moving person.

  
The plan shows each measured wall with its distance from the spot you turned on, the same number a tape from that spot would read, and a band as wide as its uncertainty. Dashed amber edges are sides the scan could not hear. While you turn, the strip at the top shows every echo as distance against heading; a wall draws a wave, and the solver's walls are the yellow curves.

  
Phones that give the browser two microphone channels (some Android phones in Chrome) also measure the direction of each echo from the delay between the microphones. In simulation that found more walls and fewer wrong ones; Details says whether your phone does it.

  
A second spot can be recorded for the field test. Fitting two spots together from the walls alone is not reliable yet, so the plan shows the first spot only.

  
Limits: each spot maps the convex space around it; stand about a metre from walls and away from big furniture; very soft rooms (curtains, sofas) echo weakly. Accuracy so far is measured in simulation only. iPhone Safari sends sound to the earpiece while the microphone is open, which makes it quieter, and gives one microphone channel.
